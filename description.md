Front end for a Router, Router with Mixer, or MediaCast Router component in a Q-SYS design. Sources on the left, destinations on the right. Two routing modes: One-To-One (source-first full matrix by default, with interlocked sources and destination fan-out highlighting) and One-To-All (one source pushed to every output). Each destination also carries a routed-source combo box that both reads the route back and sets it. Per-source and per-destination visibility pins hide entries that are not physically connected.
